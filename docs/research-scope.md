# Research Scope and Evidence Boundaries

## Primary source

The project description and reported results in this repository are grounded in the accompanying research paper:

**Generative AI-Driven Self-Healing and Adaptive Spectrum Management for Resilient Satellite Communications**

## Two complementary research tracks

### Adaptive spectrum management

- TimeGAN-based synthetic spectrum-demand and interference generation
- CNN, LSTM, and GAN-based demand modeling
- PPO-based dynamic spectrum-allocation experiments
- CNN-based interference classification
- Evaluation under 50–100% spectrum availability

### Satellite self-healing

- Blender-based synthetic satellite-damage imagery
- Binary and nine-class damage classification
- CNN + Vision Transformer damage classification
- Edge-Connect GAN damage restoration
- Feed-forward damage-to-healing strategy mapping
- GPT-2-based decision support for spectrum and repair reporting

## Evidence policy

Numbers in the README are reported values from the research paper. They are not represented as freshly reproduced benchmarks unless a new experiment has independently been run and documented.

The current research validation uses synthetic data and simulation. The repository therefore avoids claims of flight qualification, operational deployment, or demonstrated physical satellite repair.

## Future validation identified by the research

The paper identifies future work involving public ESA/NASA datasets and optimization for radiation-hardened satellite hardware.
